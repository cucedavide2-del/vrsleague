How MMR works

Win big, lose small

1 · Your result

2-0100%
2-170%
1-230%
0-20%

Points scored nudge it by up to ±5%. A forfeit counts as 2-0.

2 · What was expected

Before the match your MMR gap gives an expected result: two equal teams are 50/50, a team 200 MMR higher is expected at 76%.

You gain when you beat what was expected, you lose when you fall short.

3 · MMR change

50 × (result − expected)

Equal teams: 2-0 is ±25, 2-1 is ±10. Lose 1-2 to a team 200 MMR above you and you still gain +3.

First 3 matches of a team count ×1.5 to find its level fast.
